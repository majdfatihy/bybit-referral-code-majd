Paying on Google Play, ChatGPT and other stores

  • The virtual card works at online merchants that accept Mastercard, subject to your card region and each merchant's own rules.
  • Cashback and merchant rebates do not apply to purchases made through app stores (Google Play, App Store) or aggregators. To earn rewards, pay on the merchant's own website.

  • If a payment is declined, check the billing address shown in the card dashboard, your available Funding Account balance and the approval prompt in the app.

Fund, sell and pay with local payment methods

Bybit's funding guide offers three routes. The methods shown to you depend on your country, currency and account eligibility.

Best for local methods

P2P trading

Buy or sell USDT directly with other users. Bybit lists 80+ payment methods, including bank transfers, instant bank payments and e-wallets. The seller's crypto is held in escrow until both sides confirm.

Buy Crypto → P2P Trading → choose currency and payment method.

Fastest

One-Click Buy & Fiat Deposit

Buy crypto with a supported Visa or Mastercard and other available providers, or deposit fiat into your Funding Account and use that balance to buy crypto.

Provider fees and quotes apply and vary by country.

From other wallets

Crypto deposit

Send crypto from an external wallet or another exchange to your Bybit deposit address. Always match the coin and network, and include the memo/tag for coins that need it (such as XRP).

Assets → Deposit → copy the address.

Buy USDT with P2P, then spend or trade

  1. Open Buy Crypto → P2P Trading and pick USDT and your local currency.
  2. Filter by your payment method (for example a local bank or e-wallet) and choose a merchant with good completion rate.
  3. Place the order, pay the merchant exactly as shown, then tap Payment Completed.
  4. When the merchant releases, USDT lands in your Funding Account. From there you can top up your card, trade, or earn.
Stay safe: never release or accept based on a screenshot alone, pay only through the method listed in the order, keep all chat inside Bybit, and avoid adding sensitive words to payment notes.

Sell crypto and receive local money

Use P2P in the other direction: sell USDT and receive payment through the local methods the buyer offers. In our Arabic tutorials this includes Vodafone Cash, InstaPay and bank transfers in Egypt, plus many local methods in other countries.

  • Check that the name on the receiving account matches your verified name.
  • Release the crypto only after the money is visible in your own account.
  • Withdrawals to external wallets need your security setup (Google Authenticator, Fund Password).

Frequently asked questions

How do I use the Bybit referral code MAJD?

Register through the button on this page, or enter MAJD (or 118303) in the referral field during sign-up. Bybit states the referral relationship is created only at registration, so a code or link cannot be added afterwards.

How do I collect all the gifts?

Register through the button on this page, verify your identity, open the Rewards Hub for the Welcome Gift during your first 7 days, then finish the trading tasks before their deadlines: 14 days for the first trade, and 30 days after it for the next two. Rewards pass a 3-day risk review and must be claimed within 10 days.

What can a new user earn?

There are two parts. The Rewards Hub Welcome Gift (up to 100 USDT, unlocked in the first 7 days) and the Referral Program tasks: 50 USDT for a first trade of at least $50 within 14 days, 100 USDT at $500 volume and 100 USDT at $10,000 volume within 30 days after the first trade, paid to both you and your referrer. Rewards are first-come, first-served, pass a 3-day risk review and must be claimed within 10 days.

Do I need to verify my identity?

Yes. Identity Verification (KYC) is required to claim rewards in the Rewards Hub and to apply for the Bybit Card. Start it from Profile → Identity Verification right after registering.

Is the virtual Bybit Card really free?

Bybit's Help Center says no fee is charged for issuing the virtual card until further notice, and the card has no annual or monthly fee. Physical cards can carry a regional fee, and foreign-currency or conversion fees may apply depending on your country.

Can I pay for Google Play, ChatGPT or Netflix with it?

The virtual Mastercard works at online merchants that accept it, subject to region and merchant rules. For cashback and merchant rebates, pay on the merchant's own website: app-store purchases (Google Play, App Store) are excluded from Bybit's merchant cashback.

How can I top up with local payment methods?

Use P2P trading to buy USDT from other users via local bank transfers and e-wallets available in your country, or One-Click Buy / Fiat Deposit. Then spend or trade from your Funding Account. Open Buy Crypto in the app to see what is offered for your currency.

Is the card or Bybit available in my country?

Not everywhere. Check the Card page and Buy Crypto screens inside the app after verification, and read Bybit's terms for restricted regions. The Referral Program on this page is for Bybit Global users.

What is the difference between Referral and Affiliate?

Referral is open to every verified user: task rewards plus 20% to 30% commission for 365 days per referee, capped at $5,000 per quarter. Affiliate needs an application and approval and pays lifetime commissions on referred traders' fees, settled daily. Switching to Affiliate ends your Referral Program rewards.

How do I withdraw affiliate commissions?

In the Affiliate Portal click Withdraw, enter an amount and it moves instantly to the Assets page of your Bybit trading account. Then withdraw to your external wallet as usual.

Are crypto and perpetual contracts risky?

Yes. Prices are volatile, leveraged derivatives can lead to losses beyond expectations, and stock perpetuals are derivatives, not shares. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.
